## 🌐 1. Overview & Vision

**Dakshyam Innovations** is an industry-leading physical-digital integrated vocational training provider and software-hardware platform. We build modular, hands-on educational technology to bridge the gap between abstract academic theory and physical, verifiable vocational engineering.

By deploying robust web dashboards coupled with physical kits (sensors, ESP32 microcontrollers, actuators), we replace traditional testing models with **verifiable, telemetry-rich project portfolios**. Students learn to wire, code, and debug real systems, and receive publicly verifiable, tamper-resistant certificates that display their actual program metrics, project titles, and evaluation lead.

## 🔒 2. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Architecture

The platform enforces a strict, hierarchical, role-based access model to isolate administration, assessment, and training delivery. Below is the comprehensive role breakdown:

### A. Administrator Role (Supervisory Command Node)
Admins have ultimate control over database structures, course syllabus cataloging, trainer vetting, dynamic visual displays, and public campaigns.
*   **Access Privileges**: Complete read-write access to all data structures.
*   **Bypass Access Passcode**: `dki2026@w` (Staff administrative password for login) or supervisory terminal verification PIN `427752` accessed via the hidden supervisory overlap click trigger (clicking on the footer's copyright signature).
*   **Core Capabilities**:
    *   **Course Editor**: Author new vocational courses (or archive/retire legacy ones), including tags, descriptive text, and hardware leasing options.
    *   **Trainer Approval Board**: Moderate trainers. Instantly approve pending trainers or revoke active access if necessary.
    *   **Visual Promos**: Add, update, or delete dynamic carousel advertisement banners and promotional gallery exhibition shots.
    *   **Manual Enrollment override**: Manually enroll students into specialized college or school training hubs.
    *   **Manual User Creation**: Instantly create Trainer and Student profiles directly within the Admin panel without going through standard registration.
    *   **Master System Purge**: Permanently wipe all seeded sandbox records and clear client-cache states to reset the system safely for clean, fresh cycles.

### B. Trainer Role (Academic Lead / Educator)
Trainers are on-site or regional educators responsible for executing training camps, configuring physical lab groups, and conducting active grading.
*   **Access Privileges**: Restricted to active groups, grades leaderboards, and certificate issuance parameters.
*   **Direct Authorization Passcode**: `trainer@dki2026` (used on standard registration to bypass admin approvals and gain instant access).
*   **Core Capabilities**:
    *   **Laboratory Group Builder**: Group student candidates into physical teams to foster collaboration on physical hardware modules.
    *   **Assessment Matrix**: Assign experience points (XP) and grade physical laboratory submissions directly into the student roster.
    *   **Syllabus guides**: Step-by-step physical kit registers, connection guides, and electrical circuit diagnostics.
    *   **Branded Certificate Customization**: Fine-tune generated certificates with custom partner logos, authorized seal files, signature titles, and joint organization banners.
    *   **Verifiable Certificate Issuance**: Issue secure, serial-coded verifiable certificates to students either individually or via a bulk-generation process for the entire active classroom block.

### C. Student / Candidate Role (Learner)
Students are active participants of the technology camps who interact with hardware kits, submit code, and monitor real-time diagnostic indicators.
*   **Access Privileges**: Restricted to their personal dashboard, their peer group, and their earned certificates.
*   **Core Capabilities**:
    *   **Progress Logs**: Visual metrics tracking completed modules, course percentages, and earned experience points.
    *   **IoT Telemetry Stream**: View and configure dynamic simulated telemetry representing actual physical sensor values, relay controls, and hardware state switches.
    *   **Peer Hub**: Inspect assigned teammates and explore collaborative group submission guidelines.
    *   **Verified Portfolios**: Access, view, and print beautifully styled, verifiable completion certificates showing their actual high-water marks and project metrics.

## 🛠️ 3. How to Update & Manage Details via the Admin Dashboard

The **Administrator Console** is a powerful visual interface. To execute administrative and structural updates, follow these concrete procedures:

### A. Creating or Updating a Course Syllabus
1. Navigate to the **Admin Dashboard** and click on the **Courses** tab.
2. Click the **Add New Course** button to open the Syllabus Creator modal.
3. Configure the following fields:
    *   **Course Title**: e.g., *ESP32 Node & Sensor Telemetry Systems*.
    *   **Duration Block**: e.g., *1 Week Boot Camp* or *15 Sessions*.
    *   **Description**: Outline core learning objectives and final capstone project.
    *   **Tags**: Add comma-separated tags (e.g., `NEP Aligned, IoT, MicroPython`).
    *   **Core Features**: Specify list markers separated by commas (e.g., `ESP32 Kit, 10 Sensors, Free Leasing`).
    *   **Hardware Support Checkbox**: Check if students receive free physical microcontroller computer leasing during the module.
4. Click **Publish New Course** to live-mount it immediately.

### B. Managing Banners and Public Campaigns
To update advertisements or slideshow elements on the public landing page:
1. Go to the **Promotions** tab in the Admin panel.
2. Click **Create Promo Banner** to launch a new slideshow campaign:
    *   Provide an **Image URL** (e.g., high-quality public Unsplash link).
    *   Enter a compelling **Title & Subtitle**.
    *   Specify the **Action Target URL** (e.g., `#courses` or `#syllabus` for direct redirects).
3. Under **Exhibition Gallery**, click **Post New Photo Snapshot**:
    *   Input the event title, category, caption, and high-resolution photo URL.
    *   These appear in the public landing portfolio, illustrating real on-site classroom transformations in Tier-2/Tier-3 schools.

### C. Approving or Adding Trainers & Students
*   **Vetting Pending Accounts**: Trainers who register without the secret trainer passcode are put into a "Pending" queue. Go to the **Trainers** tab, inspect their credentials, and click **Approve Trainer** to elevate their status.
*   **Direct Account Creation**: Avoid manual signup steps by utilizing the **Register Authorized Trainer** or **Enroll Student Manually** buttons inside the Admin console. Provide their initial experience points (XP), standard profile, and credentials to let them login instantly.

### D. System Reset & Sandboxing (Master Purge)
For testing cycles or fresh deployment phases:
1. Click the **Purge Seed & User-Related Data** button at the top of the Admin panel.
2. Confirm the system warning dialog.
3. This triggers a complete purge of local storage and prompts a server-side wipe of user database models, resetting the platform back to a pristine initial seed state.

## 🎓 4. Branded Certificate Customization & Verification Engine

One of the most production-grade systems of Dakshyam Innovations is the **Verifiable Certificate Architecture**, which operates with complete dynamic branding.

### How to Customize & Issue Certificates (For Trainers/Admins)
1. Log in to the **Trainer** or **Admin Dashboard** and navigate to the **Certificates** tab.
2. Under the **Certificate Style Customization** panel, update the following fields in real-time:
    *   **Authorized Authority Title**: e.g., *Director, Dakshyam Innovations*.
    *   **Authorizer Signature Seal**: Select from modern preset digital signatures or paste an authorized verification image URL.
    *   **Central Certifying Seal Logo**: Customize the institutional seal image URL (e.g., the gold circular Dakshyam badge).
    *   **Joint Affiliation Name**: e.g., *SST College & National Skill Development Corporation*.
    *   **Joint Affiliation Logo URL**: Paste any school, engineering college, or governmental partner logo.
3. Scroll to the active student list. You can click **Issue Certificate** on an individual candidate, or click **Bulk-Generate Certified Credentials** to instantly issue secure certificates to every registered candidate.
4. Each certificate automatically receives a unique **Tamper-Proof Serial ID** (e.g., `DKI-CERT-171881`).

### How Verification Works
1. Any external stakeholder, employer, or recruiter can visit the public homepage and locate the **Public Certificate Validator** module.
2. Enter the serial code (e.g., `DKI-CERT-171881`).
3. The platform retrieves the student record, displaying:
    *   Full name, course title, and certification date.
    *   The authorized certifying signatures and joint-partnership badges.
    *   The actual capstone project details and final grades.
    *   A **Print-Ready PDF Download** trigger that dynamically formats and outputs a premium physical certificate file.

## 🍪 5. Cookies & Session Storage Compliance Policy

The platform handles user state and compliance preferences natively using secure browser cookies.

### Active Cookie Parameters
The application utilizes the following first-party compliance-friendly cookies:

| Cookie Key Name | Value Type | Purpose | Expiration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `dakshyam_theme` | `'light'` or `'dark'` | Remembers the interface color scheme to prevent visual flash on reload. | 1 Year |
| `dakshyam_cookie_consent` | `'basic'` or `'accepted'` | Tracks user's opt-in level for compliance protocols. | 1 Year |
| `dakshyam_fast_caching` | `'enabled'` or `''` | Controls advanced browser cache operations to speed up transitions. | Session |

### Dynamic User Cookie Prefs Manager
To give users complete privacy control:
*   On first visit, a bottom-floating **Cookie Consent Agreement Banner** appears asking visitors to select their level of tracking.
*   A **`[ manage cookies ]`** action button is permanently available in the page footer. Clicking this button immediately clears existing compliance preferences and re-opens the selection banner, allowing visitors to modify or withdraw consent instantly.
